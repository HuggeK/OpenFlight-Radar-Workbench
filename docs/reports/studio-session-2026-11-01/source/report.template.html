OpenFlight field report · 2026-11-01 · indoor studio · IWR6843 self-trigger

Studio Session 2026-11-01

OpenFlight on the IWR6843 self-trigger, hitting into a net beside a TrackMan 4. 127 IWR triggers over four runs, matched shot by shot against 76 TrackMan shots exported with SwingSync.

TL;DR

  • The OPS243 read 1.49 × TrackMan's ball speed from 11:37:53 until a restart at 12:35. Its capture window grew from 34 to 48 ms at the same moment: it looks like the radar dropped from 30 to ~20 kS/s mid-run. Outside that period, ball speed matched TrackMan to 0.995 ± 0.028.
  • The self-trigger caught 57 of 76 real shots on time (75 %). Six fired on the takeaway or backswing, ten never fired, three fired 60-70 ms too early.
  • False triggers are frequent and SNR does not fix them. Addresses, waggles, practice swings, placing a ball and walking up all fired it at SNR 1.5, 2.0 and 3.0; each one blinds the IWR for 7.7 s and can cost the next real shot.
  • IWR launch was 2.4° MAE (n=8), three times the project's 0.83° TrackMan baseline. --iwr6843-net-m must be unit → net; entering ball → net (3.0 m) clipped every track.
  • Real-time scheduling buys nothing: S! reaches the OPS in 0.17 ± 0.01 ms either way.

Lessons learned

  1. The OPS changed its sampling mid-run. At 11:37:53, with no host command in between, ball speed went to 1.486 ± 0.035 × TrackMan, club speed to 1.37 ×, the J3 post-trigger window from ~32 to ~48 ms, the log's post-trigger from 34.5 to 0.0 ms, and the clock sync from 36 to 59 valid readings. A restart fixed it.
  2. --iwr6843-net-m is unit → net. 3.0 m (meant as ball → net) left the tracker 0.5 m, about 10 ms of flight. Replaying the same dumps with 4.6 m tracked 24-41 ms on every real shot. Unit → net was 5.30 m.
  3. The self-trigger caught 75 % of real shots on time. Early triggers on the takeaway left the OPS reading the slow backswing (20-50 mph) and the real shot fell in the blind window.
  4. False triggers are frequent; SNR does not fix them. 20 saved shots and 41 no-ball triggers had no TrackMan shot behind them. At SNR 3.0 a practice swing still triggered and real shots went missing.
  5. Every trigger blinds the IWR for ~7.7 s with --debug: 7.1 s readback plus a radar restart.
  6. IWR launch angles were 3× worse than the baseline: 2.4° MAE, 1.4° median, +1.5° bias on 8 clean shots, with the radar 0.04 m above the floor, lower than any validated mount.
  7. Real-time scheduling buys nothing. The window jitter that looked halved was the 11:37:53 switch.
  8. “TI radar capture failed” is misleading. It shows on every shot because the firmware never has an onboard result ready; the data is still saved.
  9. Launch OpenFlight from the desktop icon. Over SSH with nohup … & the server ignored SIGINT, and SIGTERM skipped the OPS idle step.

Against TrackMan

TrackMan's clock ran 425.5 s ahead of the Pi's NTP time; at that offset 66 of its 76 shots line up with an IWR trigger within 1 s. TrackMan stamps a shot 70 ± 29 ms after OpenFlight's trigger, so a trigger more than 0.3 s before TrackMan's stamp is an early one.
